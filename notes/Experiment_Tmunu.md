# Experiment — behaviour of one T_μν, not composition

Tony Kawas / 6 September 2026. Result note. Not a paper.

Question asked: do not hunt what DE/DM are *made of*. Measure how the one stress tensor behaves on each floor.

---

## DE floor vs late-universe envelope

Data envelope used as a *box*, not a likelihood:

- Λ: w = −1
- CPL centre from DES + DESI-style combinations: w0 ≈ −0.84, wa ≈ −0.53, so
  w(z) = w0 + wa z/(1+z).
  At z = 0.3 that is w ≈ −0.96.
- Crude 68% box: Δw0 = 0.10, Δwa = 0.30 (corners, ignores covariance).

TAFA curves: same integrator as Waist_sim.md. α = 1. No new parameter.

| model | w(0.3) | inside the box? |
|---|---|---|
| data CPL centre | −0.96 | — |
| TAFA waist, f = Mpl | **−0.53** | **No** |
| TAFA 3H, f = Mpl | **+0.04** | **No** |
| TAFA waist, f = 10 Mpl | −0.96 | Yes (by hierarchy) |
| Room B1 (bare Λ0) | −1 | Yes (by giving up field-DE) |

Convention 1 fails the behaviour test. The field at f = Mpl does not act like the late-universe source the sky is naming “DE.”

That is the experiment. It is not a bottle of dark energy.
