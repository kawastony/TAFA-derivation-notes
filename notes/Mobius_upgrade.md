# Möbius upgrade — TIFA cosine to TAFA tan²

TIFA: \(V_T=\Lambda^4(1-\cos(\phi/f))=2\Lambda^4\sin^2\theta\), \(\theta=\phi/(2f)\).
TAFA: \(V_A=\Lambda^4\tan^2\theta\), \(|\phi|<\pi f\).

Identity:
\[
V_A=\Lambda^4\frac{V_T}{2\Lambda^4-V_T},\qquad w=\frac{z}{2-z},\quad z=V_T/\Lambda^4,\; w=V_A/\Lambda^4.
\]
Möbius matrix \((a,b,c,d)=(1,0,-1,2)\). Pole at \(z=2\): TIFA hilltop \(V_T=2\Lambda^4\) becomes the TAFA wall.

Same midpoint: \(V(0)=V'(0)=0\). Curvatures: \(V_T''(0)=\Lambda^4/f^2\), \(V_A''(0)=\Lambda^4/(2f^2)\).

Upgrade = cut the cosine circle at the hilltop and send that point to infinity. Forced: the map, the walls, the shared floor. Not forced: \(\Lambda,f,\mu,z\).

Inverse: \(V_T=2\Lambda^4 V_A/(\Lambda^4+V_A)\).
