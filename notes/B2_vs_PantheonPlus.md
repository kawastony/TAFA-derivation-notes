# B2 vs Pantheon+

Tony Kawas / 6 September 2026. Result note. Not a paper.

Room B2 only. No bare \(\Lambda_0\). No extra parameter.

---

## Data

Pantheon+ SH0ES distance table (`Pantheon+SH0ES.dat`).  
Unique CID, Hubble flow \(z_{\mathrm{HD}}\ge 0.023\), diagonal \(\sigma(\mu)\).  
N = 1309. Offset \(M\) floated (SN do not pin \(H_0\)).

This is a **shape** test. It is not the official Pantheon+ likelihood (stat+sys covariance not used). Relative \(\Delta\chi^2\) between models is the claim. Absolute \(\chi^2/N<1\) is expected with diag-only errors.

---

## Models

Same \(\Omega_m=0.31\), \(\alpha=1\), \(\Lambda^4=\rho_\Lambda/\tan^2(\alpha/2)\). Standard Klein–Gordon with \(3H\dot\phi\). B2 as chosen, not the waist lapse.

| model | \(\chi^2\) | \(\Delta\chi^2\) vs \(\Lambda\)CDM |
|---|---|---|
| flat \(\Lambda\)CDM | 568.0 | 0 |
| B2 \(f=10\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) | 567.5 | **−0.4** |
| B2 \(f=30\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) | 567.9 | **−0.1** |
| B2 \(f=M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) (refused by Decision_B2) | 762.5 | **+195** |

---

## Verdict

On this SN shape test, B2 at the required hierarchy is **indistinguishable from \(\Lambda\)CDM**. Planckian \(f\) is ruled out by the same data, as the behaviour test already said.

B2 is allowed by supernovae *because* \(f\gtrsim 10\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\). The data do not derive that \(f\). They permit it.

---

## What this does not do

- Replace the full SN+BAO+CMB likelihood.
- Derive \(f\).
- Touch \(a_T\) or the DM floor.
- Restore B1.
