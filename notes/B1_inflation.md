# B1 does not get a free inflaton

On B1 the well is unused at late times, so it is tempting to send it to inflation.

Near φ=0, V_A ≈ (Λ⁴ / 4 f²) φ². That is quadratic (chaotic) inflation.

For N=60 e-folds: n_s ≈ 1−2/N = 0.967, r ≈ 8/N = 0.13.
Planck+BICEP: r < 0.036. Ruled out.

Away from the bottom, tan² is *steeper* than quadratic, so r is not smaller.

B1 therefore does **not** automatically give a viable inflaton. Early universe is still empty on both rooms unless a *different* potential or a different field is introduced. That would be a third floor, not this well.
