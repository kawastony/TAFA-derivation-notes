# Integrating Paper 3 winding clock

Tony Kawas / working note, 3 September 2026
Result: the ODE as written does not close. No DM-end date.

Paper 3:

\[
\frac{\mathrm{d}n}{\mathrm{d}N}=\frac{Q(N)}{C_{\mathrm{twist}}},
\qquad
Q(N)=\sum_{\mathrm{active\ tiers}}n_i.
\]

\(Q(N)\) is not a function of \(N\) until the active-tier rule is written. It is not written.

\(C_{\mathrm{twist}}\) has two incompatible formulae; the topological derivation is an open task in Appendix C.

\(n(N_0)\) is said to be fixed by BBN. No value is given.

The threshold they do write, \(n/n_{\max}=0.627\), is *assigned* to \(z\approx 0.35\), not obtained by integrating (1).

Appendix B pause algebra with \(r_{p,\mathrm{cosmo}}=c/H_0\) implies \(H=H_0\), hence \(z=0\), not \(0.35\).

Appendix E is a kpc-scale \(P(k)\) cut, not a redshift-12 clock.

Minimal closures (\(Q\approx n\), or constant \(\alpha\)) can put first integer \(n=1\) at any \(z\) by choosing the extra constant. Fitting \(\alpha\approx 0.18\) to land on \(z=12\) is forbidden.

The DE end of the bridge stays at \(z\sim 0.3\). The DM end is not computable from Paper 3 as published.

Recommended: either derive \(Q(N)\) from the 5D action with no leftover galactic \(v_\infty\), or drop the winding ODE as a cosmic clock and retire \(z=12\) as a derived date.
