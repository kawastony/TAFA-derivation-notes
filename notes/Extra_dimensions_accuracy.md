# Extra dimensions and accuracy

Tony Kawas / 3 September 2026. Can we use the papers’ “more dimensions → better fit” here?

There are three different upgrades in the stack. Only one belongs on the floors.

## 1. Paper 2 gas-disk — yes, on the DM floor

Paper 1 (n=1, stellar-disk assumption): MAE = 0.080 dex on SPARC N=125.
Paper 2 (gas-disk / inner–outer layer, shifted reception node): MAE = 0.051 dex (~36% better).

That is **not** a new spacetime dimension. It is one extra geometric switch for extended gas. Allowed on the DM floor as an *effective* correction, same two constants Λ_*, μ, no per-galaxy μ. Do not spend a new mass on it.

Held-out test still required (DM_tests.md).

## 2. Compact n = 2, 3 axion tower — not as glue

Paper 1 Appendix C/E/L: n compact angles → n axions. Paper 4 KK: masses add, matrix diagonal.

Paper 4 then *assigns* masses by hand:

| Field | Mass | Job |
|---|---|---|
| B₁ | ~10^{-33} eV | DE / Hubble |
| B₂ | ~10^{-30} eV | clusters, z~2–5 |
| B₃ (Option B) | ~10^{-22} eV | fuzzy galactic DM |

That is the three-scale split we already measured (Storey 1), rewritten as “n compact directions.” Extra n always gives extra knobs. Accuracy that comes from adding a mass per phenomenon is a fit, not a derivation.

Paper 4 also quotes w₀ = −0.773, w_a = −0.152 from θ=π/2, f=M_Pl. Our B2 scan: f=M_Pl is *not* DE-like (goes kinetic). So that “DE from n=2” is worse than B1, not more accurate.

**Use:** KK additivity is a true statement about a product metric. **Do not use:** n=2/3 as a licence to put DE, clusters, and SPARC on one field by naming three compact circles.

## 3. 5D warp / 10D parent — already spent

5D Einstein–scalar gave Δy and the late tick z∼0.3. That is the DE floor’s extra dimension. It did **not** improve SPARC MAE and did not print 11/72 or z=12.

10D / Golden Gate: shooting targets. Retired.

| Upgrade | Use here? |

| Paper 2 gas layer (0.080 → 0.051 dex) | Yes — DM floor, effective |

Adding dimensions is allowed only when a closed equation outputs a number already on that floor. It is not a general accuracy machine.
