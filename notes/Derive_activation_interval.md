## 2. Identify \(\beta\) and \(y_0\)

5D Einstein–scalar:

\[
A''=-\frac13(\phi')^2\le 0.
\]

Take \(\beta=A'\). Then \(\beta'=A''\). The surface \(A''=-2\) (i.e. \((\phi')^2=6\)) *is* the point where \(\beta'(y_0)=-2\).

The partner surface is the wall \(\phi\to\pi f\), where \(A'\to-\infty\) and \(A\to-1/2\).
