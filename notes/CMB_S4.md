# CMB-S4 sensitivity goals

Tony Kawas / 3 September 2026. Exploration. Not a paper.

## The design target (while it was a project)

Science requirement SR 1.0, and the 2022 ApJ forecasting paper (Abazajian et al.):

- If r = 0: **r < 0.001 at 95% CL**
- If r > 0.003: **> 5σ detection**
- 4-year median: 5σ at r ≈ 0.004; r = 0.003 is ~4σ in 4 years, ~5σ in 8

That is ~30× tighter than BK18 (r < 0.036), ~100× tighter than Planck-alone (r < 0.10).

Why 10^{-3}: many high-scale / super-Planckian single-field models, and a well-motivated slice of the models suggested by n_s ≠ 1, predict r ≳ 0.001. Crossing that line either sees GUT-scale inflation or forces the remaining models onto plateaus with tiny r (Starobinsky, α-attractors).

## Status as of 2025–26

DOE and NSF ended support for the **CMB-S4 Project** (announced mid-2025). The science collaboration still exists; the $~900M Stage-4 build does not. Do not treat CMB-S4 as a scheduled measurement.

What still exists at that sensitivity class:

| Experiment | r goal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| CMB-S4 | r<0.001 (95%) or 5σ on r>0.003 | Project cancelled |
| LiteBIRD (JAXA) | δr = 0.001 including systematics | Still in definition; launch quoted ~2032–36 |
| Simons Observatory SATs | originally σ(r)~0.003; 2025 forecast σ(r)~0.0012 if r=0 | Operating / building out |
