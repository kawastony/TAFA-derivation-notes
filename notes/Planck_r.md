# Planck / BICEP r and the TAFA well

Tony Kawas / 3 September 2026. Exploration note.

r is the tensor-to-scalar ratio: primordial gravitational waves over density ripples, evaluated at a pivot k (0.05 or 0.002 Mpc^{-1}). Slow-roll: r = 16ε. Large r means a steep inflaton potential (large field excursion).

## Data (95% CL, r = 0 in the posterior)

| Data | Limit |
|---|---|
| Planck 2018 TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing | r_{0.002} < 0.10 |
| Planck 2018 + BK15 | r_{0.002} < 0.056 |
| BICEP/Keck through 2018 (BK18) + Planck + WMAP | **r_{0.05} < 0.036** |
| Planck 2018 + BK18 + BAO (n_s–r plane) | r_{0.05} < 0.035 |
| Planck PR4 + BK18 profile likelihood | r < 0.037 |
| Tristram et al. 2022 Planck PR4+BK18+BAO | r < 0.032 |

Headline number used in the floor notes: **r < 0.036** (Ade et al., PRL 127, 151301, 2021).

Planck 2018 also gives n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042 (68%). No detection of tensors. Planck 2018 X: quadratic inflation is *convincingly ruled out*.

## What quadratic (TAFA bottom) predicts

V ∝ φ²: N e-folds ⇒ n_s = 1 − 2/N, r = 8/N.

| N | n_s | r | vs r < 0.036 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 0.960 | 0.160 | ~4.4× too high |
| 60 | 0.967 | 0.133 | ~3.7× too high |
| 70 | 0.971 | 0.114 | ~3.2× too high |

n_s for N=60 is fine. r is not. The n_s–r point sits well outside the 95% contour.

TAFA V_A = Λ⁴ tan²(φ/2f) is quadratic at the floor and *steeper* toward the walls, so r does not drop. This is not Starobinsky (r ~ 0.003) or a plateau α-attractor.

## What would be allowed

Concave plateaus: Starobinsky R², Higgs inflation (same attractor), α-attractors with small r. Those are different potentials. The Möbius upgrade does not produce them.

Future (if built): CMB-S4 design goal r < 0.001 (95%) or 5σ on r > 0.003. That still would not revive quadratic TAFA; it would kill more of the remaining large-r tail.

## Floor implication

B1 cannot assign this well to inflation without contradicting BK18. Early universe stays empty on both DE rooms unless a *different* potential is introduced (third floor).
