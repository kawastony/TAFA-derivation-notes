## What this means for the line

The original TIFA/Observer idea — *one field is late acceleration* — does **not** automatically survive Baseline A with only an \(\mathrm{O}(1)\) angle.

This is a result for every TOE on the line, not only TAFA: retiring \(Q\) and the Golden Gate numbers leaves a thawing scalar that is too fast, unless you tune.
