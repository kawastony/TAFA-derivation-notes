## Verdict

SN alone could not tell \(f=10\) from \(\Lambda\)CDM. **BAO can.** High-\(z\) distances (especially Ly\(\alpha\) at \(z=2.33\)) see the thaw that \(f=10\) still has.

\(f=30\,M_{\mathrm{Pl}}\) stays near \(\Lambda\)CDM (\(\Delta\chi^2\approx 7\) on this approximate likelihood). That is the working B2 shelf if the field is DE.

\(f=10\) is no longer a pass on the joint test. The hierarchy did not go away. It got a floor.
