## What the picture is

- **Mountain** = TIFA hill (cosine peak), or the cone standing in 3D.
- **Dent at the tip** = the hilltop flattened / pinched. Möbius sends that point to infinity (the wall). Quadratic-midpoint keeps the floor of the well while the rim moves.
- **Line around the dent** = a level set / rim: φ = ±π f, or a contour on the cone.
- **Viewed flat** = unroll the cone to a sector, or replace the curved 3D patch by a disk in the galactic plane.

That picture is already on the floors as: Möbius wall + 2D disk law. It does not add a new equation.

## Where ~12 actually sits in geometry

The only clean “~12 from a dent vs a flat patch” is the sphere factor, not extra dimensions:

$$
\frac{\text{area of a 2-sphere}}{r^2}=4\pi\approx 12.57.
$$

A small spherical cap (dent on a mountain tip) has 3D area ~12 r² if you compare to r², or **4×** if you compare to the flat disk π r². Neither is SPARC MAE. Neither is 5D/10D.
